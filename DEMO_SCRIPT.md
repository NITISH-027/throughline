# 3-minute demo script (say it in your own words)

Record your screen with the app open at http://127.0.0.1:5000. Aim for 2:45 to 3:00.

0:00  HOME. "Meetings forget. Decisions get reversed, owners get lost, and nobody notices.
      Throughline is a meeting-to-execution agent that remembers across meetings."
0:15  Click "Load demo" in the top nav. "Last week's kickoff meeting is now in memory:
      launch Dec 5, Postgres, 2 lakh budget, mobile only."
0:30  CAPTURE. "This week's replanning transcript. One line per speaker, so every decision has a person.
      Speakers are detected live." Point at the speaker chips. Click Analyze.
0:50  REVIEW, conflicts. "Four things in this meeting contradict last week. Launch moved to Dec 19,
      Postgres to MongoDB, budget up, desktop added. A summarizer would miss all four."
      Click Replace on three, Keep on one (say why: for example "we still want mobile only").
1:25  ACTIONS. "Owners and deadlines are pulled out. 'next Friday' gets a yellow check-date badge.
      Priya has no deadline, flagged. The mockups task looks like an existing one, so I can update
      the old task instead of duplicating it." Click "Update TL-1 instead".
1:55  CONFIRM. Click Review and confirm. "Nothing is posted until I confirm. This shows exactly
      what goes to the tracker, what does not, and what changes in memory." Click Confirm and post.
2:15  MEMORY. "Every decision keeps its history: Dec 5 replaced by Dec 19."
2:30  BOARD. "Reminders: overdue and due soon." Change the TODAY date forward two days to show it move.
2:45  DIGEST. "A weekly summary per person." Be honest in one line: "AI is real through Gemini with my
      key; the reminders and digest stay in the app." Close with the one-line pitch.

Tips: turn the key on first (AI LIVE chip) if you can. Do one dry run. Do not claim anything the app does not do.
